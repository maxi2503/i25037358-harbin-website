TAKE A CLOSER LOOK

Places, walks
& local flavours.

A simple introduction to the sights and tastes of Harbin.

01 / A CITY WALK

Central Street

Walk along this pedestrian street to see historic buildings, browse shops and enjoy the atmosphere of the city.

02 / AN ARCHITECTURAL LANDMARK

Saint Sophia Cathedral

Look closely at the cathedral's domes, brickwork and arches. The surrounding square is a lovely place to pause and take photos.

ON THE TABLE

Try a taste of Harbin.

Three local favourites to look out for.

01

Guo Bao Rou

Crispy pork with a sweet and sour coating. A well-known dish from northeast China.

02

Harbin Red Sausage

A smoky, savoury sausage associated with the city. Enjoy it as a snack or part of a meal.

03

Madieer Ice Cream

A familiar Harbin treat. Look out for this creamy ice cream while exploring Central Street.

A SMALL REMINDER

Take your time.

Wear comfortable shoes for city walks. In winter, bring warm layers, gloves and a hat.
